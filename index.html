Before you buy a raw card

Open every listing photo and check these 4 things. This is a quick raw-card condition guide, not grading advice.

If unsure, skip it. There will be another one.
Looks good Be careful

Is the picture straight?

The card should not look pushed hard to one side. Check the front and the back photos.

Are the edges clean?

White dots or chips on the edge usually mean wear. Zoom in before buying.

Are the corners sharp?

Avoid bent, fuzzy, lifted, or crushed corners. Corners are often where damage shows first.

Can you see enough photos?

Good listings show front, back, edges, and corners. If photos are blurry, ask for more or skip.

Quick buying checklist

Front photo looks even
Back photo looks even
No white edge spots
Corners look sharp
Photos are clear

This helps avoid damaged raw cards, but it cannot guarantee a PSA 10.
